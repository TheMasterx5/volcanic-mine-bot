Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026
Operator: TheMasterx5

Operator review needed: Confirm that a private ticket or equivalent private contact method is available, and check Google Cloud logging settings before relying on this policy.

1. What this policy covers

Volcanic Mine Server Bot is a Discord bot that supports applications, complaints and moderation in the Volcanic Mine Discord server.

2. Information processed

Depending on what a user submits, the Bot processes Discord identifiers (such as user IDs and usernames), the contents of forms, complaints and reports, and related information required for moderators to review submissions. A complaint may mention another Discord member. The Bot also receives technical information required to respond to Discord interactions.

3. Purposes

This information is used to route applications and complaints to moderators, review submissions, manage server operations, detect misuse and maintain the Bot. It is not collected for advertising or sold to advertisers.

4. Where information goes

Submissions are posted to restricted Discord moderation channels. The Bot does not use a separate application database according to its operator's current configuration, but Discord messages containing submissions are stored within Discord. The Bot operates on Google Cloud infrastructure, which may process technical operational information. Discord and Google Cloud handle information under their own applicable terms and policies.

5. Who can access it

Authorized Volcanic Mine moderators and administrators with access to the relevant private channels may view submissions. The Bot operator may need access to technical information for maintenance and troubleshooting. Discord and the hosting provider may process information as platform providers.

6. Retention

Application and complaint records generally remain in private moderation channels after they have been reviewed; they are not automatically removed at case closure. Records may be deleted earlier after a valid request, administrative review, or when no longer reasonably necessary, taking into account safety, abuse prevention and applicable law. Deleting an account or leaving the server does not automatically erase earlier submissions. Operational logs, if enabled in Google Cloud, may have separate retention settings.

7. Requests and deletion

To ask about access, correction or deletion of information, open a private support ticket in the Volcanic Mine Discord server or contact the server administration privately and ask for TheMasterx5. Include the relevant Discord account and enough information to identify the submission, but do not publicly post sensitive information. Requests will be reviewed, with identity checks where appropriate; some records may need to be retained for legitimate moderation or legal reasons. Users can also exercise applicable data-protection rights.

8. Security

Access to submission channels is limited by Discord channel permissions. No security measure is completely risk free. Avoid including passwords, financial information or unnecessary sensitive data in applications or complaints.

9. Changes

This policy may change when functionality, providers or data handling practices change. The current version is published here.

10. Contact

Responsible operator: TheMasterx5. Contact privately through the Volcanic Mine Discord server's support-ticket or moderation-contact process.
